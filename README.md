I work on research problems in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, with an emphasis on making the reasoning behind the work visible and traceable.

This GitHub profile represents my personal research record. It is not a portfolio of applications or polished demos. The repositories here document research systems, experiments, failures, and evolving questions as they occur.

I am the Founder & Lead Researcher of NDX Intelligence, an independent research and documentation lab built around a simple principle: knowledge should be open, and disciplines should not be gated by background or formal labels. Work under NDX Intelligence prioritizes clarity of reasoning, explicit assumptions, and reproducibility over presentation or speed.

Most active research projects live under the NDX Intelligence GitHub organization. This profile exists to make authorship, responsibility, and research ownership explicit, and to provide a stable entry point for reading the work over time.
